Account & Data Deletion Request

At CodeChatHome, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

Please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support team.
  2. Include your username and registered email address.
  3. Our team will process your request within 7–30 days.
Email: mandelashaban593@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile information (name, username, email, phone number)
  • Learning progress and course history
  • Chat history between students and mentors
  • Account credentials and authentication data

Data Retention Policy

When a user requests account deletion in CodeChatHome, the following applies:

  • Most user data is permanently deleted within 7–30 days after the request is submitted.
  • Some limited data may be retained for up to 90 days for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance purposes.
  • After the retention period, all remaining personal data is permanently removed from our systems.

Important Notice

Important: Once your account is deleted, this action cannot be undone. Retained data (if any) is securely protected and not used for personal purposes.
